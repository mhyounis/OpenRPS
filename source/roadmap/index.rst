Developer's Roadmap
===================

.. attention:: This page is WIP.

.. OpenRPS is intended to remain in active development. In these pages, I list the major updates that I am planning for the future, across Lionbolt, NittanyPhysics, and Terpdose.

.. There is no ETA on any of these, and updates will not necessarily come in the anticipated order. Keep in mind that, at the moment, OpenRPS is largely a hobby project contributed to by only myself. If you are particularly interested in one of the updates listed in the following pages, you are welcome to reach out to me an let me know of your interest. You are also free to try your hand at implementing any of them, and I'd be more than willing to discuss the implementation with you.

.. Note finally that inbetween major updates there will be minor updates, which will involve individual instances of cleanup and refactoring of various parts of the code, and then patches where necessary.
