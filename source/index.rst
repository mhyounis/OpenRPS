.. container:: hbarorps
    
    The Open Radiation Physics Suite (OpenRPS) is a collection of projects that are focused on, at the moment, MeV X-ray and electron radiation transport, to study problems ranging from radiation therapy to astronomy. It currently consists of three major projects: 

.. image:: _static/assets/logos/Lionbolt.png
    :width: 50%
    :target: Lionbolt/index.html

.. container:: vbarlb
    
    A program/library which can perform deterministic solves of the Boltzmann transport equation on a discontinuous finite element mesh. Primarily written in Fortran.

.. image:: _static/assets/logos/NittanyPhysics.png
    :width: 80%
    :target: NittanyPhysics/index.html

.. container:: vbarnp
    
    A program/library which discretizes the cross sections of photons and electrons, at the moment exclusively under the multigroup approximation. Written in Fortran.

.. image:: _static/assets/logos/Terpdose.png
    :width: 55%
    :target: Terpdose/index.html

.. container:: vbartd
    
    A python package which provides tools to extract and post-process Lionbolt and NittanyPhysics data.
